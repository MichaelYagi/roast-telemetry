Settings

Every panel here is optional — nothing needs changing to use the app. Click Save at the bottom after any change.

Roaster safety

Limits on Heater/Fan/Drum, and the emergency fan level. See Control & Safety for the full explanation.

Temperature Unit

Celsius or Fahrenheit — display only. Storage, the API, and every threshold/config field you type into stay Celsius regardless. Downloads (.alog, JSON, CSV, Excel, PDF) follow this setting too.

Big Readout Panel & Small Readout

Pick which live values show in these two optional panels next to the live chart. Independent of each other (a value can be in one, both, or neither), but share the same colors.

Controls

Arrange the vertical control sliders (Drum, Air, Burner %, Burner SV) beside the live chart, and whether each gets +/- step buttons.

History

How many roasts load per page, and the most you can compare at once.

AI Roast Review (Ollama)

Point this at a local Ollama server to get AI-written reviews on a roast's detail page. See Analysis for what it does with it.
